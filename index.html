The concept

How it’s supposed to work

The idea is straightforward — the same physics behind morning dew, engineered into a backyard unit:

1. Draw in air

The unit pulls in surrounding air — the inventor says ordinary outdoor humidity is enough.

2. Condense moisture

Cooling forces water vapor to condense into liquid — dew, on demand.

3. Filter & collect

Collected water passes through filtration before storage, per the inventor’s design.
