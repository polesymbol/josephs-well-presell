Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Field Report collects on this site and what we do with it. Short version: as little as possible, never sold, never shared with advertisers.

What we collect

  • Email checklist signup. If you request the free 72-Hour Home Water Checklist, we collect the name and email address you enter. We use it only to send you the checklist and the occasional related note. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Anonymous analytics. We use Meta Pixel to understand aggregate visits to this site (for example, which pages are read). This does not identify you personally to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We don't share your email with third parties except the email service that delivers the checklist.
  • We don't collect payment details — purchases happen on the vendor's checkout page, which has its own privacy policy.

Affiliate links

Some links on this site are affiliate links. Clicking one takes you to the vendor's site, where their privacy policy applies from that point on.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from emails anytime via the link in any email. You can block cookies or tracking pixels in your browser settings; the site still works.
